### 3.1 North-star use cases 1. **Track your album:** Open /album, tap stickers to mark ownership; use filters (team, type, missing/owned/duplicate) to focus completion gaps. 2. **See what you can trade:** /repetidas summarizes duplicate inventory; extras implicitly appear in the mock **Mercado** listings. 3. **Find a swap partner:** Set city + lat/lng on /perfil (or use browser geolocation), then browse /sugerencias for missing stickers ranked by nearby collectors who have duplicates and might want your extras. 4. **Explore geographically:** /mapa shows mock collector pins; tap for contact badges (WhatsApp, IG, Telegram, mail). 5. **Bootstrap a demo session:** "Cargar demo" on home or album calls seedDemoCollection() (~40% ownership, ~25% of owned are duplicated) so UX can be explored without manual data entry.
