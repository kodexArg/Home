### 3.2 Non-goals - Visitor comments and social sharing (PRD.md out of scope). - Generic user accounts for the public (evolved toward gated access, but not a multi-user SaaS). - Real-time weather as a first-class product feature (weather endpoint exists as a proxy to Open-Meteo but PRD lists weather as out of scope for the product vision). - Local D1 or dev-server-as-source-of-truth workflow—production validation is intentional (AGENTS.md, README.md). - Checked luggage planning—the trip constraint is carry-on only (10 kg each).
