### 3.1 North-star use cases 1. **GM rolls a street NPC** — pick Milicia, Hombre, poder 35, click Generar; use sheet at table or screenshot. 2. **Reuse a favorite NPC** — Cargar dropdown lists all .pickle files except ultimo.pickle; reload recalculates initiative/defense from stored attributes/skills. 3. **Consult scenario during mystery arc** — open Historia, select "Ocaso Naranja" or "Sótano del Asilo", read location and NPC notes alongside generated characters.
