### 3.1 North-star use cases 1. **Quick NPC at the table** — GM sets class + poder, hits Generar, reads stats from the browser card. 2. **Named recurring NPC** — GM fills optional nombre/apellido/edad/bio fields, saves under a custom pickle filename via /guardado. 3. **Session prep with lore** — GM opens /historia?historia=<key> to read location and NPC paragraphs for a chosen scenario entry (e.g. "Ocaso Naranja").
