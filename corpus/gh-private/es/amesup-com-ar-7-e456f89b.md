### 3.1 North-star use cases 1. **Prospective borrower** lands from a social ad → reads services/FAQ → fills contact form or taps WhatsApp → commercial team receives structured inquiry. 2. **Marketing operator** updates copy, stats, or FAQ entries in landing.data.ts → CI passes → Amplify redeploys static bundle. 3. **Developer** runs npm start locally, iterates on standalone components under src/app/frontpage/, verifies with Vitest smoke test in app.spec.ts.
