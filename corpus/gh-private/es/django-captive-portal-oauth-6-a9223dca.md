### 3.1 North-star use cases 1. **Returning guest** — Connects to venue WiFi, redirected to portal, clicks Log In, authenticates via Google, sees trilingual welcome, browses freely. 2. **New guest (intended)** — Connects, authenticates, but is blocked or redirected to a reception-approval page until staff registers them (not yet coded). 3. **Operator** — Uses Django admin to manage users/accounts; eventually would pair with UniFi controller authorization.
