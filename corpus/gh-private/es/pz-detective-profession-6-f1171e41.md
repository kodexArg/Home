### 3.1 North-star use cases 1. **New game as Detective** — pick profession at character creation, spawn with iconic clothing, begin with sneak/lightfoot/aiming XP head start. 2. **Indoor clearing** — toggle vanilla search mode before opening doors; higher chance of spoken zombie warnings in small rooms. 3. **Urban loot runs** — leverage forage bonuses for ammunition, trash, and junk while navigating with +2.2 vision and weather/darkness tolerance.
