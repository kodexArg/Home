### 3.1 North-star use cases 1. **Operator dispensación:** POS clerk scans member REPROCANN, verifies monthly quota, records lot-linked handoff, updates inventory, prints differentiated receipt (UC-POS-02, UC-POS-07). 2. **Member self-service:** Authorized socio consults dispensación history, downloads certificate, requests data rectification under Ley 25.326 (UC-PS-02, UC-PS-04, UC-PS-07). 3. **Public retail:** Visitor purchases CBD oil online without seeing medicinal catalog; educational content explains REPROCANN enrollment (UC-TP-01, UC-TP-05). 4. **Compliance audit:** Inspector traces a dispensed lot from harvest through processing to final member; administrator exports semiannual Ministry report (UC-TC-02, UC-GA-01). 5. **Documentation consumer (this repo today):** Engineer or agent reads PRD + annexes + legislation to implement models, views, and compliance checks without guessing regulatory requirements.
