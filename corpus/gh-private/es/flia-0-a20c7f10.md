# Flia > **Problem thesis (required):** Flia is a private, legacy Django application for **family household economics** — tracking articles (purchases and services), who is responsible, payment method, category, and dated payment amounts. It exists to give a small household a structured back-office (Django admin) instead of ad-hoc notes or spreadsheets, with Argentine Spanish labels and a data model sketched in before implementation.
