# SDGD > **Problem thesis (required):** SDGD (Sistema de Dimensionamiento y Gestión de Dotación) exists to turn static hospital organigrams into an operational **capacity-planning system** that measures and closes the gap between **demand** (positions defining required hours and skills) and **offer** (employees with contract regimes, reductions, and assignments). It adds a **scheduling module** for cyclic monthly calendars in clinical services, **legal validation** aligned with Ley 9539 and Circular 4, and **immutable audit logging** — all on a server-rendered Astro app backed by Supabase PostgreSQL with Row Level Security.
