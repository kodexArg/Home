### 3.1 North-star use cases 1. **End user opens a request** — Select UDN → sector → accounting category (cascading HTMX partials), enter title/amount, submit; ticket starts in open status. 2. **Supervisor authorizes → technician budgets** — Role-appropriate transitions move open → authorized → budgeted with required comments and PDF budget attachments. 3. **Dual authorization and payment** — Manager and director authorize (authorized_by_manager, authorized_by_director); system may auto-insert payment_authorized; purchase manager processes payment through processing_payment, shipping, closed. 4. **Operator triage** — List view lazy-loads ticket rows on viewport intersection with hx-sync queue and staggered CSS reveal (docs/features/ticket-loading.md). Session-persisted filters (UDN, sector, status, needs attention) narrow the queue.
