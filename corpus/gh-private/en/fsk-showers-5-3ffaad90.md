### 3.1 North-star use cases 1. **Register shower use** — Operator navigates to Create, fills Responsable, Cliente, and optional Plan, submits; record is persisted and user is redirected to the list with a success flash. 2. **Review today's registrations** — Operator opens List to see ticket ID, client, responsible party, and timestamp columns (intended; see open questions for model/template drift). 3. **Correct mistakes** — Operator deletes a row via the delete link on the list page.
