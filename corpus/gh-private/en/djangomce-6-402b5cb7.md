### 3.1 North-star use cases 1. Staff logs in, sees current floor announcements on the home tablón, and reads important notices filtered by date range. 2. HR user creates a novedad (e.g., vacation) — form shows only the fields required for that TipoNovedad, attaches optional file, and lists entries in a paginated exportable table. 3. Operations analyst with estadísticas permission opens "Producción por día" to view color-graded daily coin-in and win aggregates for the last N months. 4. Facilities staff opens the temperature chart, selects frequency and day range, and zooms Highcharts splines per RPi sensor.
