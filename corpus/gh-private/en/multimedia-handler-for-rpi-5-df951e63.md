### 3.1 North-star use cases 1. Operator uploads a promotional still, sets 15 seconds at 1920×1080, selects three RPi device folders, submits — ffmpeg runs, MP4 copies land in each folder, players sync within minutes. 2. Operator opens the Videos page to verify which clips are staged per device before a campaign goes live. 3. Operator uses the copy modal (partially wired) to duplicate a clip’s staging path reference toward another device folder.
