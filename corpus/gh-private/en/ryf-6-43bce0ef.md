### 3.1 North-star use cases 1. **GM at the table** — Open /personajes, skim the card fan, double-click an NPC, reveal biography then secret for plot hooks. 2. **Author pipeline** — POST new characters to /api/characters/ (via DRF or Django admin), drop portrait PNG into static/img/c/, refresh the deck. 3. **New player onboarding** — Read /sistema for how SyV maps RyF attributes and optional extended sheets before session zero.
