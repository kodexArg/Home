Gabriel publishes ready-to-use deployment templates: Astro 7 with Django 6 DRF on AWS ECS Fargate, Django on AWS App Runner, self-hosted n8n on App Runner, an AWS Lambda that updates Route53 DNS records from EC2 events, and an Angular 21 starter with PrimeNG, Tailwind v4 and Vitest. They are the reusable distillate of the infrastructure he runs in production.
