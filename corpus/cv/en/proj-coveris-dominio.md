On Coveris the business logic came from physicians, not from Gabriel. He worked directly with clinical professionals translating their domain rules into verifiable, traceable specifications, with automated QA validating every contract against those rules. He describes it as the pattern he wants to repeat: someone else's expert domain, his architecture and quality.
