kodexArg is Gabriel Cavedal's personal brand and organization. It covers his own domain and its subdomains, and the GitHub organization of the same name, where he publishes open-source repositories: developer tools, AI agent frameworks, IoT automation scripts, cloud infrastructure templates and application source code.
