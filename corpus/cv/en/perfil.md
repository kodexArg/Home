Gabriel Cavedal is an infrastructure and software architect and a multi-company technical lead with over 20 years of experience. He designs systems end to end, from the physical layer and networking up through cloud architecture and product, and leads the teams that operate them. His current practice is spec-driven development with fleets of AI agents under BDD/TDD discipline. He is a Django and Django REST Framework expert and runs production platforms on AWS and Cloudflare. He presents professionally as an AI Solutions Architect and Fractional CTO, and is based in Mendoza, Argentina.
