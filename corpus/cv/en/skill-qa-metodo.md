Gabriel went deep on quality assurance applied to AI-assisted development. His argument is that when agents write most of the code, quality moves to the specifications, the contracts and the review. His method: specifications and ADRs as the single source of truth, contract-first APIs with test-verified coverage, BDD/TDD, and multi-agent audit pipelines with models in an Opus/Sonnet/Haiku hierarchy cross-reviewing code, specs and documentation. He applies the same process as an external reviewer of third-party projects: architecture audits, code review and technical debt assessment. He documents with docs-as-code, using mkdocs Material as living documentation.
