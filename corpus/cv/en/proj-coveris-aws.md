Coveris is a hospital capacity planning SaaS and the project where Gabriel demonstrates the most depth on AWS. The MVP runs on AWS Amplify for the frontend, ECS Fargate for the services and RDS PostgreSQL, with Cognito for authentication, over an Angular 21 with PrimeNG and Django 5.2 with DRF stack. The architectural design is entirely his. The repository is private, so there is no public link.
