Welp is a ticketing and purchase-to-payment workflow system built with Django 5, HTMX and AWS App Runner, running in production at Grupo ALVS. It covers ticket lifecycle tracking, SLA management and multi-org isolation. The source is public as the welpdesk repository; the production instance is internal to the group.
