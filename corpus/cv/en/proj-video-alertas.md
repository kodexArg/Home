Gabriel built a Linux-on-ARM system that connects to any RTSP camera and sends motion alerts — stills and video — to a Telegram group. It runs in production. The stack is Python, OpenCV, RTSP and python-telegram-bot. The related public code is camera-alert-to-telegram.
